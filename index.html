Moksha Patam

The old Indian game of snakes and ladders, played as one life. Grow from a baby through the four ashramas: student, householder, forest-dweller and renunciate. Virtues lift you up; vices drag you back to a younger self. The first to pass the realms of the gods and reach Moksha at 110 is liberated.

Brahmacharya Grihastha Vanaprastha Sannyasa
