# Moksha Patam

The old Indian game of snakes and ladders, in a single HTML file. Ladders are virtues that lift you up and snakes are vices that drag you down. Climb past square 100 into the realms of the gods; the first to reach Moksha at 110 is liberated.

**Play:** https://codebpr.com/moksha-patam/

- 2–4 players, each human or computer
- Snakes and ladders named for vices and virtues, with their Sanskrit terms
- A heavenly top row (101–110) of deities ending in Moksha
- Optional rules: exact roll to finish, and a 6 gives another turn

To play locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
