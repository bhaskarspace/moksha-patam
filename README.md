# Moksha Patam

The old Indian game of snakes and ladders, played as one life, in a single HTML file. Grow from a baby through the four ashramas (student, householder, forest-dweller and renunciate). Virtues lift you up; vices drag you back to a younger self. The first to pass the realms of the gods and reach Moksha at 110 is liberated.

**Play:** https://codebpr.com/moksha-patam/

- **The board as a life:** squares 1–25 are Brahmacharya, 26–50 Grihastha, 51–75 Vanaprastha and 76–100 Sannyasa. Above them is a row of divine realms ending in Moksha.
- **Ladders are the virtues of each stage:** Shraddha, Vinaya, Vidya and Satya for the student; Dharma and Dana for the householder; Tapas and Vairagya for the forest-dweller; Jnana at the end.
- **Snakes are the inner enemies:** the six shad-ripu (Kama, Krodha, Lobha, Moha, Mada, Matsarya), plus sloth, falsehood, hypocrisy, fear of death and, last of all, Ahankara (ego).
- **Characters that grow:** pixel-art players walk the board and grow from baby to toddler, student, youth, householder, forest-dweller, renunciate and divine soul. A snake sends you back to a younger self.
- **Landing cards:** special squares zoom up into a card with the Sanskrit name and a teaching, mostly from the Bhagavad Gita.
- **Animations:** ladders are climbed rung by rung, and snakes swallow you whole and spit you out at the tail.
- 2–4 players, each human or computer. Optional rules: exact roll to finish, a 6 gives another turn, and turning the cards on or off.

To play locally, open `index.html` in a browser. Add `?debug` to the URL to get test helpers in the console: `mp.jump(n)` moves the current player and `mp.roll(v)` rolls a fixed number.
